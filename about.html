CARE (Chidambaram Academy of Refinement and Enrichment) was started on 2006 and named after the renowned freedom fighter, "Mr.V.O.Chidambaranar of thoothukudi". The main objective of CARE is "Societal Development through Human Resources Development".

To be a world class institution to impart the value and humanity based real life and professional education to all.

To enhance the employability of youth through coaching, counseling, mentoring and training.
