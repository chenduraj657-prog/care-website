Mr. Bala Chander

Joint Director – CARE

Bringing with him over 40 years of industry experience, Mr. Bala Chander plays a pivotal role in shaping the growth and reputation of CARE. As Joint Director, he has been a driving force behind the institution's brand building, industry engagement, and corporate collaborations, ensuring that CARE remains aligned with evolving professional standards.

Through regular industry interactions, partnerships, and knowledge-sharing initiatives, he bridges academia with real-world practices, creating meaningful opportunities for students and organizations alike. His vision and leadership continue to guide CARE towards becoming a future-ready, industry-relevant, and socially responsible institution.

Dr. Butchi Babu Muvva

Zonal Director – CARE (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh)

Dr. Butchi Babu Muvva is a dynamic and visionary leader providing strategic guidance to advance education, human resources, and corporate services across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Guided by the principle of "Societal Development through Human Resource Development," he has played a pivotal role in strengthening industry—academia collaborations, fostering skill development, and promoting CSR-driven initiatives that create meaningful impact in regional communities.

He actively integrates Artificial Intelligence and data-driven approaches to enhance learning, workforce development, and organizational effectiveness. His forward-looking approach continues to inspire organizations and individuals to align their goals with a broader vision of progress, humanity, and societal impact.

AI-Enabled Practices Skill Development CSR Initiatives Industry—Academia Inclusive Growth
"Leading Societal Transformation through AI-Enabled Training & Development."

Director

Mr. N. Pandian

M.A., M.Phil. (Safety), C.Yoga., CTPM – Director, CARE

Mr. N. Pandian is a multifaceted professional with expertise as a Certified PoSH Trainer, Industrial Safety Trainer, Yoga Trainer, Publisher, Motivational Speaker, and Tamil Poet. With a strong academic foundation and extensive practical experience, he has conducted numerous programs on workplace safety, risk management, and compliance, while also promoting holistic well-being through yoga practices.

As a publisher, he contributes knowledge-driven books to society, and as a motivational speaker, he inspires confidence, positivity, and resilience among students and professionals. His Tamil poetry reflects cultural pride and deep human values. By integrating safety, wellness, creativity, and inspiration, Mr. N. Pandian empowers both individuals and organizations to achieve excellence while fostering personal and societal growth.
